# Note taking, everything together: test checklist

The coach's notepad from start to finish (decisions 96 and 97), as a coach would use it at a session.
Accounts: **Coach_J** (coach) and **Piero** (rows).

## 1. Set it up
1. Blue **+** → **Land session** → **+ Add piece** → **500**, **Reps 4** → **Done**. **Set** top right.
2. **+** → **Land session** with **no pieces**, Title "Free row". **Set**.
3. **+** → **Land session**, turn on **Test day**, choose **2k**. **Set**.
- [ ] Each one shows under Workouts → Today, with a thin **Open notepad ›** row.

## 2. Writing scores (the 4 × 500m)
Tap **Open notepad**.
- [ ] One row per rower, in erg order, with **4** columns.
- [ ] Typing **1 4 4 . 6** shows "1:44.6".
- [ ] **Next** moves down to the next erg, then to the next piece, skipping anyone sat out.
- [ ] **Sat out** marks that piece for that rower.
- [ ] Typing, **+ Row**, **+ Column** and **Notes** all respond straight away, with no lag.
- [ ] Leave the page and come back: everything is still there.

## 3. The number of columns follows the pieces
- [ ] "Free row" has **1** column.
- [ ] Workout → **Edit** → add a piece → **Save**. The notepad gains the new columns and keeps your scores.

## 4. Scanning a monitor (please send a screenshot if this fails)
Tap the camera button by a rower's name.
- [ ] The first time, iPhone asks for the camera. Then the **live camera shows** in the frame.
- [ ] If it can't start, a **red message** says why, with **Try again** (and **Open Settings** if camera access is off).
      **Screenshot that message for Claude.**
- [ ] Photograph the results screen. The pieces read appear, and new ones are highlighted.
- [ ] **Scan the next screen** on a long result: pieces seen twice are kept once, and the count goes up ("14 of 20 read").
- [ ] **Fill [name]'s row** fills their row, and the numbers match the monitor.
- [ ] The photo isn't kept anywhere.

## 5. Many pieces and tests
- [ ] A workout with many reps: **Summary | Every piece** both work, and a row opens to its pieces.
- [ ] In **Every piece**, the names stay fixed while the pieces scroll sideways, and there's no dead space.
- [ ] On the 2k test: time, split and rate per rower, and a row opens to its 500m splits when they were posted or scanned.
- [ ] A timed piece takes metres and works out the split.

## 6. Text, tables and notes
- [ ] **Add text** and **Add table** add blocks. Table headings can be edited, and **+ Row** and **+ Column** work.
- [ ] **Notes** stays at the bottom of the page.

## 7. A rower's own post
1. Sign in as **Piero** on another phone and post the 4 × 500m with **Link this session** on.
- [ ] On the notepad, Piero's row fills with a small blue dot.
- [ ] Changing a score there changes only the notepad, not Piero's post.

## 8. The Notebook
Workouts → **Past** → **Notebook ›** (only workouts whose day is over).
- [ ] **‹ ›** and swiping turn the pages. **Splits | Against target** both work, and green is faster, red slower.
- [ ] **All squads ⌄** filters by squad, and **Edit page** opens that notepad.
- [ ] Tapping a name shows that rower's line across the notebook.

## 9. What the rower sees (Piero)
- [ ] The Feed shows **"Your coach noted your scores"**, with only their own scores.
- [ ] If they haven't posted: a **Log your session ›** row, and Log opens with nothing filled in.
- [ ] They never see the coach's text, tables, notes or other rowers.
- [ ] Their volume, PBs and Rankings don't change from anything the coach noted.

## 10. Another coach
- [ ] A second coach in the club sees and can edit the same notepad.
