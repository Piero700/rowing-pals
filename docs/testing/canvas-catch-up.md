# Canvas catch-up — test checklist

Decisions 36–38 in `docs/design/v2-decisions.md`. No migration. The push function `send-push` was
already redeployed to staging without quiet hours. Use the staging build on your phone, signed in
as a rower **who is in a club and has a gender and level set** (Edit profile).

## 1. Rankings → Volume opens on your group
- [ ] Tap **Rankings**. Expect under the lilac card a glass pill reading your group, e.g.
      **"Senior men · My club · Erg + water"**, and **"N rowers"** on the right. The list shows
      only rowers of your gender and level in your club.
- [ ] The lilac card's top line reads **"Weekly volume · You · #N"** (your place) and the bottom
      line **"Group leader · Name · metres"**. If you're first: "You lead this group".
- [ ] Tap the pill. Expect a sheet: **Reset · Filters · Done**, then **Gender** (Men / Women /
      All), **Level** (Senior / Novice / All), **Rowers** (My club / Following), **Source** with
      "Volume only" (Erg + water / Erg / Water), and the line "Opens on your own group: … in
      <your club>."
- [ ] Pick **All** for Gender and Level, tap **Done**. Expect the pill "All rowers · My club ·
      Erg + water" and more rowers. Open the sheet, tap **Reset**: back to your group.
- [ ] Pick **Erg** under Source: the list re-sorts by erg metres and the pill ends "· Erg".
- [ ] Leave Rankings and come back after closing the app: it opens on your group again.

## 2. Rankings → Test results
- [ ] Tap **Test results**. Expect **BEST IN YOUR GROUP** with your group on the right, then two
      gold cards, **2K · BEST** and **5K · BEST**: the fastest rower of your gender and level in
      your club, their time, and one of "You're 6.5s behind", "Fastest in your group" (if it's
      you), "No result from you yet" or "No results in your group yet".
- [ ] Then **YOUR RESULTS** and the usual grid of tiles.
- [ ] Tap a gold card: that test's board opens, already filtered to your group (pill at the top).
- [ ] A rower with no club sees no gold cards, just the grid.

## 3. Settings
- [ ] **Profile → ⚙︎ Settings → Appearance**: three rows, **Dark mode**, **Light mode**,
      **Match iPhone**. Pick **Light**: the app turns light at once. Pick **Match iPhone**: it
      follows the phone. Open Control Centre (swipe down from the top right), long-press the
      brightness slider, tap **Dark Mode** on and off: the app follows each time. Put it back
      how you like it.
- [ ] **Notifications**: three switches, no **Quiet hours** row.
- [ ] **Help**: Contact us, Terms of Service, **Privacy Policy**. Tap Privacy Policy: a page
      with What we collect, What happens on your phone, Who can see it, Where it's kept, Deleting
      your data, Contact. **Read it and tell Claude anything that's wrong or missing.**

## 4. Alerts arrive normally at night (needs a second phone or account)
- [ ] Late in the evening (after 22:00), have someone comment on your post. Expect a normal
      alert with sound — no more silent delivery.

## 5. Workout screen
- [ ] Open any photographed post. The **SESSION TOTAL** card has no "✓ Photo-verified" label.
      A manual entry still shows "Logged later" where it applies.
