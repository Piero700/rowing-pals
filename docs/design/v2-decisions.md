# v2 redesign — decisions

Decided 2026-09-23, resolving the open questions in `rowing-pals-redesign-handoff-v2.md` §6.
These override the prototype wherever they differ. The user can change any of them at any time;
if they do, update this file.

| # | Topic | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Posts | Keep the full multi-post feed history. Do **not** copy the prototype's "one live post per user" behaviour. |
| 2 | Prediction / estimate cards (2k, 5k) | Build the card UI. The user has an existing prediction algorithm and will supply it; integrate it when provided. Until then, show the card as an explicit placeholder with no invented numbers. Do not write your own algorithm. |
| 3 | Units | Session and post distances stay in **metres**. Add a metres/km toggle **for leaderboard totals only** (weekly/monthly/yearly volume gets large). Storage stays whole metres (`distance_m`); conversion happens at the view layer. |
| 4 | CSV export | Not wanted. Omit the "Export your metres" row. Everything stays in the app. |
| 5 | Palette | Adopt the four-accent-role palette (brand / records / rank / success) as in CLAUDE.md. |
| 6 | Private accounts and follow requests | Build them, as an **early phase** (schema: `profiles.is_private` plus follow-request status; review every place `follows` is read). |
| 7 | Clubs | Build as in the prototype: create club, join policy (open / approval / invite), roles (owner / co-owner / admin / member), join requests, owner-only management. Supersedes the "club model open question" note. |

**Resolved:** redesign Phase B (commit `e47755c`) built the metres/km toggle app-wide; commit
`903698c` narrowed it to the Volume leaderboard only, per decision 3.

Other prototype items are placeholders and need the user's go-ahead before building: quiet hours,
the demo-data seeding button (dev-only, never ship). ("Who can comment" was settled by decision 33:
no setting.)

## Photos, lead piece and PB posts (decided 2026-09-25)

| # | Topic | Decision |
|---|---|---|

| 12 | New PB | Only an **actual** new PB (beats the previous best) is highlighted: the post gets an attention-grabbing animated glow (RGB-LED style). Needs its own design token(s) and a Reduce Motion fallback (static glow). |

## Clubs, v3 §12 and phase G (decided 2026-09-29)

| # | Topic | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | Clubs | **Hub** "Your crew" is v3 §12 (club name, Find a club to join, Create a club, Continue without a club), plus your role, **Manage club** for admins and up, a pending request you can cancel and invitations to accept. **Roles are tiered**: admins answer join requests, invite and remove members; co-owners also change roles and edit the club; the owner can do everything, including making co-owners. Nobody changes or removes someone at or above their own role. **Joining**: open clubs join at once; approval clubs send a request (one pending at a time); invitation-only clubs need an **in-app invite** (Invite a rower) **or the club's invite code** ("Have an invite code?"). **Owners** can't leave or switch clubs until they **hand over ownership** (they stay on as co-owner) or **delete the club** (everyone in it is left club-less; sessions and metres stay theirs). Membership changes only through server functions (`docs/migrations/2026-09-30-clubs.sql`); a trigger blocks any other change. **Club directory**: every UK university and college boat club and the UK's established membership clubs (302, `docs/migrations/2026-09-30-club-directory.sql`), open and unowned; the early placeholder clubs are removed while empty; the user owns UEA Boat Club. |
| 26 | Club follow-ups (user notes, 2026-10-04) | **One club at a time**: in a club, Your crew shows the club (details, your role) and **every other member** under "Crewmates · N" — not yourself (user, 2026-10-04) — each opening their profile, with **Leave club** as the only way out (owners hand over or delete in Manage club); the find/create buttons appear only without a club. **Requests**: after asking, the button reads **Requested** (greyed) with **Refresh**; a declined request stays visible as **"You weren't accepted to <club>"** with **Request again**; when accepted while waiting, the club screen closes back to where you started. **Invitations**: the invited rower gets an alert (push, once enabled) that opens Your crew, and the invitation also shows on their Profile; a declined invitation shows as **"Declined the invitation"** in Manage club, with Invite again. **Live updates**: club pages refresh themselves through Supabase Realtime, plus on returning to the app and by pull to refresh. A membership change made elsewhere (an admin accepts, removes or promotes you) also reloads Feed, Rankings and Profile (`MembershipMonitor`), and while a request waits the Feed says "Request sent to <club>". Server: `docs/migrations/2026-10-04-club-updates.sql`. |
| 27 | Tapping the tab you're on (user, 2026-10-04) | Tapping **Feed**, **Rankings** or **Profile** while that tab is already showing scrolls it to the top, then refreshes it, with a small spinner under the header while it reloads. The scroll finishes before the reload starts (starting both at once cancelled the scroll and hid the tab bar on a phone). `FloatingTabBar.onReselect` → `scrollsToTopOnReselect` in `DesignSystem/ReselectScrollModifier.swift`. |
| 28 | Custom tests are club tests (user, 2026-10-04) | A club's **admins and up** add tests for their club under Rankings → Test results (the handoff's trailing **"+ Add test"** tile): a distance in whole metres (100–100,000), or a time typed in **minutes or seconds** (10 seconds to 120 minutes; seconds added 2026-10-05, e.g. a 30-second test). The name is made from it in the standard tiles' style — **750m, 3k, 1500m, 20min, 30s** (not the prototype's "20 min", to match the existing 4min/30min/60min tiles); a whole number of minutes is named in minutes, so 120 seconds and 2min are one test. Timed pieces may stop 2 s or 1% from the target, whichever is bigger, but never more than 5% (1.5 s on a 30s test). A standard test or a test the club already has can't be added. **Every member** sees the club's tests after the nine standard ones, can pick one as the Session type when posting (same exact-distance / time-within-tolerance check), and each has its own leaderboard, scored fastest time or furthest distance. Admins and up can **delete** a test from its board, which removes its results. Club results never appear in profile PBs or club rankings. Server: `docs/migrations/2026-10-04-club-tests.sql`. |
| 29 | Profile pictures (user, 2026-10-05) | Tap **your own picture on Profile** (it carries a small camera badge) → **Take photo** (front camera first) or **Choose from library**, both with iOS's square move-and-scale; **Remove photo** when you have one. The picture is checked like posted photos, squared to 600 px, stored in a private `avatars` bucket and shown in **every avatar in the app** (feed, rankings, crew and member lists, comments, people lists) via `AvatarStore`; without one, initials as before. Visibility follows the photo rule: a private account's picture shows only to its approved followers. Server: `docs/migrations/2026-10-05-seconds-tests-and-avatars.sql`. |
| 30 | Pace Engine inputs and outputs (user, 2026-10-05) | Replaces "no weight class anywhere". **No weight classes or age groups in any ranking.** Bodyweight and age are private inputs to the prediction algorithm only: never shown on a profile, never visible to other rowers (stored apart from the public profile row). The engine's **weight-adjusted** and **age-graded** scores are **not shown anywhere**. The **Population Estimate** (cold start from age/sex/weight) stays **off**: a rower with no history sees the Insufficient Data state. Its values were fitted from the Concept2 rankings **medians** on 2026-10-05 (engine v1.4, SPEC.md §7.6 — user's choice of percentile), and the user chose to keep it hidden for now ("Not yet"); turning it on is `PredictionService.showsPopulationEstimate`. Note: with that off, profile age and weight don't change any prediction yet — the engine anchors on the rower's own sessions, and uses demographics only for the cold-start estimate. |
| 31 | Pace Engine in the app (user, 2026-10-05) | **Zones replace the "Main workout label"**: the review screen's **Zone** picker offers UT2 · Steady state, UT1 · Harder steady state, AT · Threshold, TR · Race pace, AN · All-out; choosing a test pre-selects AN (changeable). The zone is the main split's badge (a test keeps its own name) and is stored in `sessions.zone`; old posts map Threshold→AT, Test/"… test"→AN, Intervals and Recovery get none. Optional **"How hard did it feel?"** (CR10 0–10) in `sessions.rpe`. An interval workout's **rep distance** is read off the monitor title ("8x500m/1:00r" → 500) into `segments.rep_distance_m`. **Date of birth** (not age) and **weight** in Edit profile, private (`athlete_private`). Predictions show on the **Profile 2k/5k cards**, as a line on the **Rankings → Test results tiles**, and in a **"How we got this"** sheet from ⓘ — **not** on the PB history screen. The engine package is `Packages/PaceEngine` (phase 1: every golden vector passes). |
| 32 | Comments thread and sharing (user, 2026-10-04; built 2026-10-05) | **Comments thread:** a full-screen **Comments** screen (back button, "Name · workout" under the title, every comment oldest first, the "Say something…" composer pinned to the bottom, press and hold someone else's comment to report it). The feed card's **comments pill opens it** (as in the v3 prototype; the photo and workout link still open the workout). On the workout screen, the last 3 comments stay inline and **View all N** appears once there are more; the workout and its thread share one live list. A short thread starts at the top; a long one opens on its newest comments. **Sharing (user's choice: image card):** the feed card's share button opens **Share workout** — a preview of a 4:5 image (1080 × 1350 px) of the post's first carousel photo (the lead piece's monitor photo; an environment photo if there is none; plain ground for a manual entry) with "Rowing Pals", the workout and date, the rower's name and the **lead piece's** distance, time and /500m (decision 16, always as a split), always in the dark palette — then **Share** opens the iOS share sheet with that image. Replaces the old one-line text share. |
| 33 | Who can see and comment on a post (user, 2026-10-05) | **No "Who can comment" setting.** You can comment on (and react to) a post if you can see it, and you can see a post only if it's yours, or you **follow the author** (accepted) or are **in their club** — narrowed by the post's own Following / Club / Everyone setting, by private accounts (approved followers only) and by blocks either way. ("Friends" was settled as "either follows"; a rower the author follows but who doesn't follow back can't see the post, so it adds nothing.) **Enforced by the database**, not just the app: reading posts, their pieces, photos (monitor, gallery, selfie), comments and reactions, and adding comments and reactions — `docs/migrations/2026-10-05-post-visibility.sql`. Before this, any signed-in rower could read every public rower's posts through the API and comment on any post. A refused comment reads "Only people who follow this rower or share their club can comment." Rankings data (test results, daily totals) keeps the private-account rule. |

| # | Topic | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 34 | Coaching (user, 2026-10-05) | Build the canvas's Coaching section (`docs/design/v4/Coach*.dc.html`, `Rower*Coaching.dc.html`, `RowerPractice.dc.html`) in phases. **Coach is separate from club roles**: "Make coach" sits in Manage club's member sheet; being a coach gives no member-management powers, and being an admin no coaching powers. A club's coaches see **every member's training in that club, even private accounts**: sessions, photos, tests, PBs, effort ratings, predictions, age and bodyweight. Members are told before joining a club that has coaches ("Joining a club with coaches"), and Settings → Profile visibility repeats it. **Coach-only accounts** ("I'm a coach" at onboarding, or Account type in Edit profile): no gender or level, no Log button, never on a leaderboard or in Crewmates; they reach Coaching from Your crew once a club makes them a coach. Coaching has three tabs — Rowers, Workouts, Practices — plus Squads (filters and workout targets; a rower can be in several; coaches still see everyone). |
| 35 | Age and bodyweight for coaches (user, 2026-10-05) | **Amends decision 30.** Age, bodyweight and W/kg are visible to **the rower and their club's coaches only**; nobody else, and still never on a public profile or a ranking. | 39 | The coaching brief (user, 2026-10-05) | The user's brief "Build the Coach section" (decisions 1–6 there) is adopted alongside the canvas; where they differ, the brief's rules win and the canvas's look wins. **Granting coach:** the owner and co-owners, from Manage club → a member → Make coach; a coach can hold any club role. **Non-rowing coaches** never appear on any leaderboard, test board, club ranking or in Crewmates; Your crew lists **all** coaches (rowing ones too) in a separate Coaches section. **Squads:** coaches, the owner and co-owners create, rename and delete squads and add or remove members. **Entry:** a Coaching button in Your crew **and** on Profile → Club opens a full-screen area; the tab bar is unchanged. **Coach privacy** is enforced in the database. **Attendance:** the rower answers in advance (Going / Can't make it, optional reason) and can check in on the day; the coach's Present / Late / Absent / Excused always wins, can be changed later including past practices, and every mark keeps who set it and when. **Flags:** the engine's `elevated_recent_effort` and `tier_disagreement`, and no session in **10+ days**. **Workouts:** optional test day pointing at a standard or club test; posting while Today's workout shows links the session (on by default); "New workout from your coach" is a new push kind. **Practices:** one-off or weekly; editing or cancelling a series notifies rowers; an optional reminder the rower can turn off. **Server:** one migration, `docs/migrations/2026-10-05-coaching.sql`, creating every coaching table in Phase 1 (later phases update the same re-runnable file if needed). |

Where the ordinary rule already lets them see a post (a public clubmate, or they follow the rower), they can comment as anyone else. |
