Boats drawn as rowing shells

Every boat in the app, small (Boats tab, Saved boats, Add a boat, the rower’s Feed) and large (crewing): a racing shell from above — long and narrow, pointed at both ends, decks fore and aft, the bow ball, wing riggers and oars with spoon blades. Stern at the top by default, so a crew reads stroke first, top to bottom. Turned round like this, stroke side is on the right, as the rowers see it facing the stern.
