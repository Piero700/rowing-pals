Draft · your boat on the Feed, on its side

Like the single: the boat lies on its side, stern on the left and the bow (with its ball) on the right. Stroke-side oars point up and bow side down, as the crew sees it facing the stern. Every seat has its mark and the rower’s first name under it; your seat is filled in blue and your name is blue, and the headline says it in words. Boats too long for the card scroll sideways: the card opens centred on your seat, the edges fade where there’s more, and a thin bar shows where you are. A single stays as it is (just the boat, no names). The coach’s views keep the upright drawing.
