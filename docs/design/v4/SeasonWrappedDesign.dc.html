Season Wrapped

Decision 92. A rower’s season as six story pages, each shared as a 1080 × 1920 image: your season, totals, streaks, personal bests (records lilac), club and crew, training mix. Seasons are the club’s terms (from the Calendar) or your own dates; Profile → Season Wrapped any time (“so far” while a term is on), and a Feed card for three weeks after a term ends.
