Challenges and erg events

Decision 91. Coaches, the owner, co-owners and admins set challenges — a personal target, a club total or squad vs squad (metres per rower), on erg metres on the leaderboards between two days — and erg events, a set piece rowed in a window with one board (photographed pieces only; “Count for …” on the post screen). They live in Rankings → Challenges, on a Feed card while they’re on, and in the coach version’s + and Calendar. The editors and Feed cards are as built in the app.
