Design rationale

A little more fluid.

Charcoal surfaces, blue controls and subtle lilac and amber accents. Sample data lets you explore the flows.

1
Four stable destinations

Feed, Rankings and Profile share one glass bar. Log sits separately on the right.

2
Focused logging

Capture and review use dedicated controls without competing navigation.

3
One component system

Uniform cards, buttons, fields, spacing, type and states.

4
Comfortable to use

Semantic controls, keyboard focus, clear labels and generous touch targets.
