# Posting copy

Character counts are shown for every limited field (YouTube title ≤100, description ≤5,000; Instagram and TikTok captions ≤2,200). Hashtags: 3–5 per platform. Every caption keeps the "fictional" note: the videos use a demo club.

**Posting windows (general benchmark guidance, not your analytics).** YouTube Shorts: Tue–Thu, 11:00–16:00. Reels: 06:00–09:00 or 19:00–23:00. TikTok: evening, when your audience is online. For UK student rowers, try 19:00–22:00 UK time, after evening outings. Stagger platforms by an hour or two rather than posting all three at once. Once each account has history, its own analytics override this.

**Sound.** Each MP4 carries an original house track (124 BPM, A minor) generated locally, with no licence needed. To use a trending sound instead, add it in the app at posting time, at low volume under the original, or replace the original. This copy can't see what is trending this week.
