Short answers

I've forgotten my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? under the password box and follow the steps.

My confirmation email hasn't arrived

Check your junk folder first. If it still isn't there, email us from the address you signed up with.

The app didn't read my monitor properly

Rowing Pals reads the Concept2 PM5 monitor. Frame the screen fully and take the shot again, or correct the time, metres, split and rate by hand before you post.

My session isn't on the leaderboard

Only sessions with a live monitor photo count, and only with Include on leaderboards switched on when you post. Sessions entered by hand or from your photo library still count towards your own totals and streak.

Who can see my posts?

You choose for each post: Friends, My Club or Everyone. Friends are people you follow who follow you back. Your club’s coaches can see your training too, and you’re told before you join a club that has coaches.

Someone is bothering me or has posted something wrong

Every post and comment has a Report control, which sends it to us for review. You can also block someone from their post, after which neither of you can see the other’s posts.

My club isn't listed

Create it. Anyone can start a club and invite their squad, and the club’s owner can apply for the verified tag.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and everything you’ve posted. If you can’t sign in, email us from the address on your account.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhones running iOS 26 or later.
