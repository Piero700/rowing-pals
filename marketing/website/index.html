Questions

Good to know.

Do my rowers need to pay?

No. Rowers use Rowing Pals free. Only the coach subscribes to unlock the coaching tools.

Which ergs does it work with?

Rowing Pals reads the Concept2 PM5 monitor. Frame the screen, take the shot, and the time, metres, split and rate fill themselves in. You can correct anything before you post.

Why can't I upload a photo from my camera roll?

That's the point. Every session is shot live in the app straight after the piece, which is why the leaderboards can be trusted.

Where are my photos read?

On your phone. The numbers are read on-device, so your photo doesn't have to be sent anywhere to be read.

Who can see my bodyweight and age?

They're never shown on your profile or used to group a leaderboard. They sharpen your predictions, and your club's coaches can see them to plan your training. The app tells you this when you join a club with coaches.

Can I row and coach?

Yes. Switch between rowing and coaching in one tap from your profile or settings.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Manage or cancel your Coach subscription in your Apple account settings. You keep coaching until the end of the period you've paid for.
