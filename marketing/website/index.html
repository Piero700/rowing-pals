Questions

Good to know.

When can I get it?

Rowing Pals is coming to the App Store later this month. You’ll need an iPhone running iOS 26 or later.

Which ergs does it work with?

Rowing Pals reads the Concept2 PM5 monitor. Frame the screen, take the shot, and the time, metres, split and rate fill themselves in. You can correct anything before you post.

Do I have to take a selfie?

No. Turn off Selfie and just shoot the monitor, for one session or for good in Settings. A live monitor photo counts on the leaderboards either way.

Can I add a session I didn’t photograph?

Yes. Enter it by hand or choose a photo from your library. It counts towards your own totals and streak, but not the leaderboards, because the numbers can’t be checked against a live photo.

Where are my photos read?

On your phone. The numbers are read on-device, so your photo doesn’t have to be sent anywhere to be read.

Who can see my posts?

You choose for each post: Friends, My Club or Everyone. Friends are people you follow who follow you back. Your club’s coaches can see your training too, and you’re told before you join a club that has coaches.

Who can see my bodyweight and age?

Only you and your club’s coaches. They sharpen your predictions, and they’re never shown on your profile or used to group a leaderboard.

My club isn’t on there. What now?

Create it. Anyone can start a club and invite their squad, and the club’s owner can apply for the verified tag.

Can I row and coach?

Yes. Switch between rowing and coaching in one tap, and the app opens in the one you used last.
