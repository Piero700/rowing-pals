## What's real and what isn't

- **App screens** come from your design canvas (claude.ai/artifact/42YP5UcaQKWnfQdERRgLcc),
  rendered in Chromium, not captured from a phone. The type is Liberation Sans standing in for
  SF Pro. Before rendering, everything the shipped app doesn't have was removed, checked against
  the Swift code on `main`:
  - the Calendar tab, "Trained with", attendance, the @username, and the coach tab bar
    (Workouts / Boats / Calendar)
  - prediction advice lines the engine doesn't produce
  - the old "Main workout label" and pieces block on Review
  
  The leaderboard switch was set to blue (decision 44). The workout rank line uses the built
  format.
- **All names, the club and every number are fictional:** Wexmoor UBC, with Rory Ellison, Kit
  Mallory and others. The film says so on screen whenever app screens appear. The numbers are
  consistent across scenes:
  - 2k 6:42.9, split 1:40.7, rate 30
  - weekly volume 61,400 → 66,400 m
  - a 3,501 m gap: the app shows the difference + 1 m
- **The erg monitor and the front-camera silhouette are illustrations**, not photos. There is no
  filmed footage: the cinematic shots (flywheel, split, horizon, notebook) are motion design.
- **Narration:** ElevenLabs, voice "Oliver – Warm British Narrator", model eleven_v4, take B of
  four. Word timings come from ElevenLabs Scribe.
- **Music:** original, generated by `audio/score.py` (no samples, nothing to license).
- **Sound effects:** Mixkit (Mixkit Sound Effects Free License), from the video-shotcraft
  plugin's library. Sources are in `CREDITS.md`.
- **Fonts:** Inter, Caveat and Share Tech Mono (SIL Open Font Licence), bundled in
  `remotion/public/fonts`.
- **Remotion** is free for individuals and companies of up to three people; larger companies
  need a company licence.

## Rebuild

```
# screens (only if the canvas changes): see screens-build/README.md
python3 audio/score.py          # score.wav
python3 audio/mix.py            # remotion/public/audio/mix.wav + mix-nomusic.wav (needs ffmpeg)
cd remotion && npm install
npx remotion studio src/index.ts                        # preview and edit
npx remotion render src/index.ts LaunchFilm out/RowingPals-LaunchFilm-1080p30.mp4 --props='{"bgm":true}'
npx remotion render src/index.ts LaunchFilm out/RowingPals-LaunchFilm-1080p30-nomusic.mp4 --props='{"bgm":false}'
```

In this cloud container, add `--browser-executable=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium_headless_shell-1194/chrome-linux/headless_shell`.

To change a line of narration, regenerate it in the ElevenLabs flow
"Rowing Pals launch film narration". Then update its `src` / `at` in `voice/timeline.json`,
re-run `python3 voice/cut.py`, then `mix.py`.
The picture's timings are in `remotion/src/timeline.json` and the scene files.

## Before publishing

- **Listen to it.** The mix was measured (−14 LUFS integrated, −1.5 dBTP) but nobody has heard
  it yet.
- On launch day, change the end card from "Coming soon to iPhone" to your App Store CTA
  (`src/scenes/End.tsx`) and the last narration line.
- For a version with real device footage: swap the PNGs in `remotion/public/screens/` for screen
  recordings (Remotion `<Video>`). The layouts already take a 1179-px-wide screen.

## Changes from the approved storyboard (`PLAN.md`)

- **No filmed footage** (your call: none available, and stock is blocked here). Shots 1–5 are
  motion design: a drawn flywheel that surges on each stroke, an illustrated monitor, the split
  and 2k as rolling numerals, a lone light on a dark horizon, an early-start clock, a long-piece
  progress line, and a notebook of scores that blurs away. Shot 14's crew footage is replaced by
  three club phones.
- **Rankings** shows the Volume board and the rank change ("…m to move into #1" → "You lead this
  group"). The Test results "Best in your group" card was left out to keep one idea per shot.
- **Feed**: a 🎉 reaction lands (12 → 13), the comments pill highlights, then the post opens to its
  comments.
- **PBs**: the PB line draws itself, the "49.7 faster" pill is highlighted, then the Profile's
  "Predicted today" 2k card.
- The narration is as approved, word for word.
