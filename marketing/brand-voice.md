# Brand Voice & Marketing Preferences: Rowing Pals

Copy this file over the marketing-skill plugin's `references/brand-voice.md` in a new session.

## Priority Task
Short-form promo videos (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) ahead of the iPhone launch.

## Content Types
Short-form vertical video first; captions and posting copy with them.

## Target Audience
UK university and club rowers, 18–24, novice and senior, plus their coaches. They train on the
erg, live in squad group chats and know 2k / split / rate vocabulary.

## Brand Voice & Tone
Dry, teasing, inside-joke humour from inside the boathouse; competitive but friendly. Lowercase
captions on TikTok are fine. UK English (metres). Use the app's own words: split, rate, piece,
session, erg, novice/senior. Never "pace", "cadence" or "rowing machine".

## Default Output Format
All three platforms, 15–30 s, three hook variants per video.

## Banned Words & Phrases
- "game-changer", "revolutionary", "seamless", "unlock", "supercharge"
- "viral" (until analytics show it)
- any claim about user numbers, downloads or testimonials we don't have
- "download now" (until the App Store listing is live)

## Formatting Constraints
- Emoji: allowed in social captions, one or two
- Every video with app screens shows "Demo club · fictional rowers and data" while it uses
  fictional data

## Sonic Identity
- Genre: house
- Tempo: 124 BPM
- Key: A minor
- Energy: 4 (of 5)
- Hook: 1 3 5 3 | 6 5 3 1
- Why: training-session energy for an 18–24 fitness audience. Proposed by Claude, **not yet
  approved by ear**.

## Visual Identity
- Accent: #91B8FF (brand blue: CTAs), #C6ADFF (personal bests), #EFC37C (#1 / crown)
- Background: #101114
- Text: #F7F8FC
- Font: Inter (SF Pro, the app's font, isn't available for web rendering)
